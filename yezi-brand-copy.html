Our Beginning

A Vision for Meaningful Play

YEZI is a trademark of Shenzhen Yezi Kuajing Ecommerce Limited, filed in January 2018. The brand covers a wide range of children's toys including educational toys for teaching music principles to children, electronically operated toy motor vehicles, infant toys, and more.

What started as a vision to bring joy through purposeful play has grown into a trusted name in children's entertainment and education, inspiring countless families around the world.

Established 2018

Growing from a simple idea into a globally recognized brand dedicated to creating toys that matter.
